Difficulty

Touch

  • Move mode (default): drag to scroll, tap a unit to select, tap ground or an enemy to order
  • Select mode: tap the round button, then drag a box around units. It flips back to Move after
  • Pinch to zoom, or drag the minimap
  • All / Infantry / Vehicles / Idle grab a squad in one tap
  • Double-tap a unit to grab all of its type on screen
  • With units selected, hold on the destination, then drag to set which way the formation faces
  • Long-press a build button to cancel
  • Medics heal infantry, the Repair Bay fixes vehicles parked beside it, Engineers capture enemy buildings or fix yours
  • Select a building for Repair and Sell
  • Flak Nests, rifles, lancers and light vehicles can hit aircraft. Tanks and cannons can't
  • A charged superweapon shows a Launch button: tap it, then tap the target

Mouse & keys

  • Click to select or command, drag to box-select
  • Double-click a unit to grab all of its type on screen
  • With units selected, right-drag from the destination to set the formation's facing
  • Middle-drag, right-drag with nothing selected, or WASD to scroll, wheel to zoom
  • Right-click a build button to cancel
  • Esc clears selection or placement
